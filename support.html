How to sign in

Sign in with the mailbox email and password your admin set up for you. If you do not know your password, ask your ZorroMail admin to reset it. Admin and dashboard logins do not have a mailbox and cannot sign in to this app.

Common questions

I forgot my password. Ask your admin to reset it, then sign in again.

My mail is not showing. Pull down on the inbox to refresh. Check that you have Wi-Fi or data.

How do I attach a photo? Tap the attach button when you write mail, then pick a photo or file.

How do I set an auto reply? Open the menu, tap Automations, then turn on the reply you want.
