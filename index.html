ZorroMail

Your work email, right on your phone.

ZorroMail is a clean and simple email app for your work mailbox. You sign in with the email and password your admin gave you. Then you get your whole inbox on your phone. Read your mail, write new mail, and keep things sorted with ease.

What you can do

  • Read your mail with folders for Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Spam, and Trash
  • Write new mail with To, Cc, and Bcc, plus files and photos
  • Reply, reply all, and forward in one tap
  • Star mail you want to find later
  • Search your whole mailbox fast
  • Keep a contact book and block bad senders
  • Set auto replies for office hours, quick replies, and time off
  • Add your name, a signature, and a profile photo
